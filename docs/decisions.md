| 15 | 2026-11-16 | Images whose description marks them as non-T1 (localizer, scout, survey, calibration, FLAIR, T2) are removed from the T1 manifest before one image per session is chosen | The completeness check found a 9-slice localizer (MCI) and a FLAIR (CN) chosen over the real T1 of the same session, because they were not marked "accelerated"; the fix changed only those 2 images, no subjects or splits | Farrukh (delegated to Claude) |
| 16 | 2026-11-16 | Every preprocessed scan is checked by eye on its QC picture, not only the flagged ones. Scans that are misaligned are redone with multi-start registration (`--retry`: starting tilts and heights; `--retry-wide`: also every 90-degree orientation), then checked again | The 0.30 score cut-off missed slight tilts (scores 0.38 to 0.47) and flagged some aligned scans with weak contrast or strong atrophy; only the eye check is reliable | Farrukh (delegated to Claude) |
| 17 | 2026-11-16 | Image-quality rule: a scan whose motion, ringing, streak or noise artefact covers brain tissue fails QC, even when aligned. A failed scan is replaced by another T1 from the same session if one exists and passes QC; otherwise the subject is removed from its cohort and split (never moved). Artefacts outside the brain only (for example a line in the scalp) are accepted | Artefacts change brain texture and could bias predicted brain age; clean training data was preferred over a few extra subjects | Farrukh (delegated to Claude) |
